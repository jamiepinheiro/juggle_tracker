    What to box

    • Every ball you can see: in the air, in a hand, partly hidden, blurred.
    • Fit the box around the ball, including its blur streak.
    • No balls in view? Save it empty. That teaches the model what isn't a ball.
    • Not sure? Skip. A wrong box hurts more than a missing one.
